Questions

What happens if I stop paying?

The domain is registered in your name from day one, so it stays yours. We hand over DNS control within five working days, and export your form data as a spreadsheet.

Can I buy the site instead of renting?

Yes. Buying outright runs 2,000–4,200 ₾ depending on the package. After twelve paid months the buyout price drops, reaching 60% at two years.

Do I need to understand any of this?

No. You know your business, we handle the website. We ask for your text and photos and do the rest.

How fast does it go live?

Setup takes five working days once we have your content. Changes after that are two to three working days depending on your package.
